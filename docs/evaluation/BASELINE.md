## Reading these numbers

- Five synthetic scenarios written by the model's author: construct bias is the main threat to validity (blueprint §26.6). The positive rate is 11.9%, below the 15-25 % the blueprint aims for, and A_high_cvss_unreachable contributes 44 of the 52 negatives.
- Scenario E's SSRF is reported by two Semgrep rules on the same line; both are labelled important, so it counts twice.
- No KEV entry appears in the snapshot for these CVEs, so KEV does not influence any method here.
- This is the baseline later phases must beat; the numbers are not tuned. The pre-registered targets are in `docs/proposals/EXPANSION_A-D.md` §6.
